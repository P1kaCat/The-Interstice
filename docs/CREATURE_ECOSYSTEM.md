# The creature family (ecosystem)

The walls are inhabited by one family of related organisms. The **Adherent** (v5, see
[CREATURE.md](CREATURE.md)) is the dominant species and the anatomical template; seven related species
share its body plan (forward-leaning humanoid frame, very long forelimbs, gripping hands with three
fingers and a thumb, digitigrade hind legs, scapulae under the skin, a small eyeless wedge head with a
hidden mouth) and differ in proportions, posture, gait, behaviour and role.

Status vocabulary used below: **tested** = covered by a GameTest (`./gradlew runGametest`);
**captured** = seen in the in-game captures in `docs/creature/family/` (stills and GIFs);
**not verified** = implemented, no automated test and no capture.

## Species

| Species | Size (blocks, rest: length / height) | Hitbox | Model (relative to the Adherent) | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Adherent | 3.32 / 1.84 | 1.4 × 1.8 | the template itself (v5) | dominant, most of the horde; indifferent unless hunting |
| Crawler | 2.08 / 1.15 | 0.9 × 1.0 | 0.62 scale, longer flexible limbs splayed sideways, low profile, dark skin | small, agile, nervous; dashes and freezes; flees or hides when fallen |
| Strider | 4.47 / 2.51 | 1.5 × 2.6 | limbs 1.75–1.9× longer, narrow torso, raised body, pale skin, tendon lines | long-distance traveller; huge deliberate strides; slow, difficult recovery |
| Burden | 4.07 / 2.36 (2.86 wide) | 2.2 × 2.4 | 1.3 scale, limbs and torso 1.4–1.6× thicker, massive shoulders, short sunk neck | heavy; high inertia; the structure answers its weight (sound, dust, vibration) |
| Clinger | 3.65 / 1.50 (2.56 wide) | 1.6 × 1.2 | flattened wide torso, limbs splayed flat, hooked fingers 2× longer, concrete-mottled skin | stays attached for minutes; mistaken for the architecture; holds on during the Stampede |
| Hollow | 3.53 / 1.87 | 1.1 × 1.7 | everything 0.5–0.7× thinner, ribs showing, longer spine and neck, pale | fragile, irregular, easily agitated; loses its grip; flees rather than hunts |
| Watcher | 3.41 / 2.32 | 1.3 × 2.3 | long neck held almost vertical, elongated head looking forward, heavier chest | observation; stillness; slow imperfect head tracking; notices the Stampede first |
| Fractured | 3.89 / 2.05 | 1.5 × 1.9 | left arm 1.4×, right arm 0.8× and thicker, raised left scapula, twisted spine, rolled head, uneven legs, healed seams | asymmetric lurching gait with a sideways drift; dangerous on the ground |

All eight are the same entity class (`AdherentEntity`) with one entity type each
(`interstice:<species>`, spawn eggs `interstice:<species>_spawn_egg`). Species parameters live in one
place: [`Species.java`](../src/main/java/com/interstice/entity/species/Species.java) (body, wall style
and speeds, Stampede weight/speed/fall/survival, ground disposition weights, voice, horde sprite).

**Adding a ninth species**: add an enum constant with its traits, add its entry to `SPECIES` in
`tools/creature/species_generator.py`, generate and export it, add its config weights. No system has a
per-species code path except the optional `WallStyle` behaviours.

## Models and animation

- Generated by [`tools/creature/species_generator.py`](../tools/creature/species_generator.py) from the
  Adherent template: per-bone morph (length along the bone, thickness across it, joint shifts), rest
  bends (posture), automatic planting on the ground (the shortest limb touches, IK flexes the others),
  species palette and surface patterns. Same 24-bone skeleton and bone names as the Adherent, so the
  exporter, the runtime skinning and the animator are shared. Editable sources: `models/<species>.bbmodel`.
- 12 clips per species: idle, observe, search, turn, walk, run, climb, cling, rear, threat, fall,
  recover (the Adherent has the same set minus search), with species parameters (cadence, stride, stance, gait phase offsets, lifts, body motion,
  head steadiness, observation amplitude, recovery time). Examples: Crawler walk 1.1 s cycle, stride
  4.6 px; Strider walk 3.4 s, stride 13 px; Burden heavy body drop on every step; Hollow hitch in the
  gait and irregular breathing; Watcher steady head while walking, 32° head turn with a 16° tilt when
  observing; Fractured asymmetric phase offsets and per-limb lifts.
- Time convention: cycles over a 0..1 phase, one-shot clips (observe, rear, threat, recover) in seconds.
- Contacts are IK-solved per key and validated between keys at 240 Hz (`models/<species>_validation.txt`):
  worst hand/foot height error in walk/run/climb is between 0.15 and 0.85 px (Adherent: 0.24–0.63 px).
- States used in game: idle, observe (stare), search (head low, sweeping), walk/run (distance-driven),
  climb/cling on walls, rear + threat (hunt start), fall, recover (landing), stunned (recover pose held
  prone with a twitch), hide (cling pose on the ground), turn (standing while the body yaw changes). Not
  authored: a separate landing clip (the start of recover is the impact).
- Turning in place ("turn"): planted hands and feet counter-rotate round the support centre while the
  others step round (16 degrees of sweep per stance, 22.9 degrees of yaw per cycle; the IK gets a
  sideways degree of freedom at the shoulder and hip for this clip). The animator drives it by the body
  yaw actually turned (forwards when the yaw grows, backwards when it shrinks), blends it in place of
  idle above about 0.3 degrees per tick, and leads with the head into the turn. Validated like the other
  clips (planted contacts within 0.04–0.37 px of the ideal rotated position, 240 Hz); the Adherent's
  clip is generated from the unmorphed template and merged into `models/adherent.bbmodel` without
  touching anything else in that file (`models/adherent_turn_validation.txt`). Captured:
  `docs/creature/family/<species>_turn.gif` (90 degrees one way, 60 back; in the frames the planted near
  hand stays in place while the body turns over it).

## Wall locomotion (server)

Constrained wall movement as before (2D (y, z) plane, X re-solved against the real blocks each tick,
easing over reliefs), now with species parameters
([`AdherentEntity.wallStep`](../src/main/java/com/interstice/entity/AdherentEntity.java)):

- **Inertia**: wall-plane velocity with a species acceleration (Crawler 0.5, Adherent 0.15, Burden 0.03:
  a Burden takes seconds to get going and glides to a stop).
- **Styles**: DART (Crawler: dashes and freezes, sharp direction changes), LONG_STRIDE (Strider: long
  journeys along the corridor), HEAVY (Burden: vibration), STILL (Clinger: 2–6 minutes idle, rare slow
  repositioning, rare sudden jerk), IRREGULAR (Hollow: uneven pace, hesitations), WATCH (Watcher: stares
  at the player with an aiming error, re-aims late, sometimes looks past the player at something
  unseen; tested: aims land on average 1.4 blocks off the player's eyes, and some looks go 40+ blocks
  beyond the player in its exact direction), LURCH (Fractured: long-short rhythm and a sideways drift).
- Tested: every species attaches and stays on the real wall surface for 200 ticks (a creature may be in
  transition over a relief, but never off its surface for more than 10 ticks).

## Behaviour and interactions

- Startle (player within 9 blocks): species-specific stare-or-flee (Clinger 85 % stare and holds for 10+ s,
  Watcher 60 %, Crawler 10 %).
- Neighbour reactions every 20 ticks (bounded box query, staggered by entity id): smaller species give
  a Burden room (tested: Adherent, Crawler, Strider, Hollow, Watcher and Fractured placed 4.5 blocks from
  an idle Burden all move 3+ blocks away; a Clinger next to it does not move); Crawlers slip around larger
  bodies; Hollows are agitated by movement nearby and, rarely (2 %), lose their grip; Clingers make way for
  nothing. Gathering prefers the same species. Tested: a Hollow flees from a neighbour crawling past it
  and stays put beside a motionless one. Not tested: the Crawler side-step.
- Burden: while moving, every 22 ticks a low step, falling dust and a noise event (heard by ground
  creatures). Tested.
- Director: new action BURDEN_MOVES (something heavy starts to move out of sight; heard and felt before
  it is seen). The existing STARE action keeps "several creatures look toward an unseen location".
- Ground dispositions, rolled per individual on landing from species weights (tested): HUNTER (full
  brain), INVESTIGATOR (searches, then watches from 6+ blocks, backs away, never attacks; tested),
  FLEER (runs from the player, then climbs back; tested), HIDER (goes to the foot of a wall and stays
  pressed and still, flees if found; tested: from 9 blocks out to the wall foot, still for 120 ticks, flees
  when a lit player steps up to it), STUNNED (lies prone for 11–21 s × species factor, then gets up as an
  investigator or a fleer; tested after a real fall: did not move or react to a lit player 2.5 blocks away
  for the whole stun, never hunted). Forced survivors (the guaranteed searcher of a Stampede) keep the
  HUNTER brain; `forceDisposition` overrides the roll (tests).

## Known limitations

- The seven new species are morphs of one template: they share topology and texture layout, and their
  differences are proportions, posture, patterns and motion. No species has extra or missing limbs.
- Textures are generated development textures (palette and patterns), not final art; see
  [PIXELLAB_ASSETS.md](PIXELLAB_ASSETS.md) batch G.
- No custom shader, normal or roughness maps; the creatures use the vanilla entity pipeline, so the
  dimension fog and lighting apply to them as to every entity.
- Contact validation covers the authored clips, not crossfades or slopes; a very fast turn (several
  degrees per tick) plays the turn steps quickly rather than switching to a different gait.
- Not covered by automated tests: the Crawler side-step around larger bodies, the Hollow's grip loss
  (2 %), the Watcher's late re-aim timing, and every client-side animation layer (checked in the
  captures only).
